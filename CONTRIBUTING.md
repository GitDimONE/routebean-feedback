# Maintaining feedback and the public roadmap

1. Review reports through the private triage inbox. Keep `bug` / `enhancement`
   as report kinds. Prepare any proposed public response or status change for owner review.
2. After owner approval, acknowledge the user in their chosen language where
   possible and explain any approved duplicate or declined status.
3. Propose real, reviewed public product requests for owner approval before
   adding `roadmap`. After approval, use at most one
   of `planned`, `in-progress`, `shipped`. Add `planned` only after a product
   decision; mark `shipped` only after release. Do not publish dates by default.
4. Keep private reports and implementation repositories private. Never mirror
   private issue bodies, attachments, internal links, or repository names. If a
   private request warrants a public entry, deliberately write a safe summary
   with the reporter's consent; the private report remains private.

## Publication and roadmap review

The existing [RouteBean roadmap](https://github.com/users/GitDimONE/projects/2)
remains empty. Do not add entries, change public statuses or comments, or publish
roadmap/release content without the owner's explicit confirmation of the exact
content and destination. Implementation approval does not grant publication approval.

Error reports are reviewed through the private RouteBean agent triage inbox, where reviewed
sanitized evidence receives Jev assistance. Only evidence-backed candidates become
private fix proposals for owner review. An AI recommendation does not authorize
implementation, closure, labels, comments, release notes, or roadmap publication.
Maintainers may review and respond through the existing feedback source after
owner authorization. No automated public triage workflow is installed.
